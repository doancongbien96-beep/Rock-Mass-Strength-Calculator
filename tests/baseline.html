Engineering notes

Equivalent Mohr–Coulomb parameters are stress-range dependent. Changing General / Tunnel / Slope / Custom changes σ′3max and therefore c′ and φ′. This does not change the non-linear Hoek–Brown parameters mb, s and a.
The calculator implements the Generalized Hoek–Brown criterion (Hoek, Carranza-Torres & Corkum, 2002), tunnel/slope stress-range recommendations, and the Hoek–Diederichs (2006) rock-mass modulus equations. Use laboratory and site-specific data whenever available; picker values are guidance, not substitutes for testing.
